Privacy Policy for App Launcher

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 | Application Package: com.cleanlauncher.app

Executive Summary: App Launcher is a privacy-first, ultra-clean Android home launcher. It does not collect, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal data, application usage patterns, device identifiers, or biometric information. All processing occurs 100% locally on your device.

1. Information Handling & Zero Data Collection

We respect your fundamental right to digital privacy. Because App Launcher operates completely offline without requiring an account, cloud login, or remote backend server:

  • Zero Personal Information Collected: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Zero Analytics & Telemetry: The application does not integrate Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Segment, Flurry, or any third-party behavioral telemetry SDKs.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: App Launcher contains no advertisements, banner ads, interstitial trackers, or advertising IDs.

2. Purpose and Usage of Device Permissions

App Launcher requests only the standard permissions strictly required to perform its core responsibilities as an Android home screen launcher:

  • Package Visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): As an Android Home Screen / App Launcher (android.intent.category.HOME), the app queries the local package manager to discover installed applications so you can categorize, search, filter, and launch them. This package list is read strictly into device memory and is never transmitted off your device.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to deliver timely user-scheduled app reminder notifications and Text-to-Speech voice alerts (e.g. daily routine or attendance reminders). Alarm schedules are stored exclusively in local SharedPreferences.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ (API 33+) to post the reminder notification banner when an alert triggers.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to re-register active user alarms with the Android AlarmManager after your device is restarted.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide gentle haptic feedback during reminder buzzer events.

3. Local Device Storage

User preferences (such as selected layout mode, custom categories, category ordering, and reminder timestamps) are stored locally in private application sandbox storage (SharedPreferences). When you uninstall the application, all local preferences are completely removed by the Android operating system.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for all audiences.
